Our Origins

Oodi College of Applied Arts & Technology (OCAAT) was established as a premier technical institute designed to bridge the gap between creative artistry and industrial technology in Botswana.

Situated in the vibrant village of Oodi, our campus was built to foster digital creators, technicians, and innovators who drive forward modern economy standards in software, multimedia, design, and applied sciences.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in applied arts and technology

Our Mission

Oodi College Of Applied Arts And Technology exist to provide training to our future artists and technicians who can drive the economy of the country through exploration, discovery and creation
