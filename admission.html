Future Starts.

Explore the detailed admission pathways for our specialized departments. At OCAAT, we nurture the technical mastery required to lead Botswana's industrial and creative landscape.

Multimedia Production

The Multimedia Production program is our most dynamic creative hub. We seek applicants who possess a unique blend of artistic vision and a hunger for digital proficiency. This course is designed to dismantle the barriers between traditional art forms and modern digital delivery through intensive practical workshops.

From your first semester, you will be immersed in high-end cinematography, professional photography, and motion graphics design. The curriculum focuses heavily on narrative structure, visual ethics, and industrial-grade branding. We don't just teach you how to use a camera; we teach you how to control the visual culture of the next generation.

Admission requires a demonstrated interest in content creation. Graduates from this department currently lead the charge in Botswana's film industry, digital marketing firms, and international advertising agencies.

Information Technology

Our ICT department is the engine room of digital innovation. We look for students who are natural problem solvers and logical thinkers ready to architect the backbone of our digital economy. The program offers deep specializations in software engineering and complex network architecture.

In our advanced labs, you will move beyond the basics, working with cloud infrastructure, data analytics, and the critical field of cybersecurity to build secure, scalable systems. Our methodology emphasizes real-world application, ensuring that students can troubleshoot high-pressure environments effectively.

Graduating as an OCAAT ICT expert means having the technical agility to thrive in the world's most demanding tech environments, from government database management to private sector fintech startups.

Sports & Recreation

The Sports and Recreational studies module is dedicated to those who see sport as a catalyst for national development. Admissions are open for individuals with a passion for physical excellence, leadership, and community health management. Our comprehensive curriculum balances the science of human kinesiology with the business of professional sports.

You will gain hands-on experience in coaching methodologies, athlete rehabilitation, and large-scale event coordination. We analyze Botswana's sporting landscape to find innovative ways to drive performance and wellness across the nation. This includes modules on sports psychology and the economic impact of recreational facilities.

Graduates are prepared for professional careers in national sporting federations, elite fitness management, and community health consultancy, filling a vital gap in our professional sports ecosystem.

Furniture Design

Furniture Design and Manufacture at OCAAT is where artisan skill meets industrial automation. We seek students who appreciate the structural beauty of materials and have the patience for precision manufacturing. The program teaches the full lifecycle of a product: from the initial CAD conceptualization to the final industrial finish.

You will master joinery, ergonomics, and sustainable material sourcing, creating furniture that is both functional art and a commercial asset. We emphasize Botswana-centric design, using local aesthetics to create world-class exportable goods. Students are also trained in the operation of CNC machinery and modern finishing techniques.

Our graduates enter the industry as designers and production managers, capable of running modern furniture factories or launching high-end bespoke studios for the international luxury market.

Jewellery Design

The Jewellery Design and Manufacture program is our tribute to Botswana's mineral heritage. We look for individuals with a high degree of manual dexterity and a keen eye for detail, ready to transform raw materials into cultural treasures. Students are trained in traditional metal-smithing alongside cutting-edge 3D prototyping.

You will learn to work with precious metals and stones, understanding both the chemical properties and the aesthetic demands of the fine jewellery market. The module focuses on value-addition, ensuring our students can compete on a global stage. This includes gemology labs where you learn to grade and identify various precious minerals.

Careers in this field range from gemological analysis to luxury brand manufacturing, providing a direct pathway into the most valuable sector of Botswana's economy.

Applied Lab Science

Precision is the core of our Applied Laboratory Science department. We seek scientifically minded applicants committed to the rigorous protocols of research, diagnostics, and industrial testing. The curriculum provides master-level training in microbiology, clinical chemistry, and laboratory management systems.

Students work with sophisticated diagnostic equipment in a sterile environment, learning to provide the accurate data that national health and industrial systems rely upon. We focus on ethical research and the latest advancements in diagnostic technology. You will be trained in quality control and standard operating procedures (SOPs) essential for accredited labs.

Graduates are the silent heroes of our health and research sectors, working in government laboratories, pharmaceutical firms, and environmental testing facilities across the Southern African region.
